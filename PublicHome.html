Admission requirements

Prepare the documents for your intended grade level. The Admissions Office will confirm any grade-specific or scholarship-related conditions.

Nursery & Kinder 1

  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Three (3) 2"x2" photos, white background, with nametag
  • Long brown expandable envelope, clean and free of writings or markings
  • Original parental / legal guardian consent, signed

Kinder 2 & Grades 1–6

  • Original official report card
  • Original Form 137 / Permanent Record
  • Original certificate
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Three (3) 2"x2" photos, white background, with nametag
  • Long brown expandable envelope
  • Original parental / legal guardian consent, signed

Junior High School

  • Original official report card
  • Original Form 137 / SF10
  • Original certificate
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Three (3) 2"x2" photos, white background, with nametag
  • Long brown expandable envelope
  • Original parental / legal guardian consent, signed
  • For applicable scholars: TLC Transfer Form (TLC scholars) or ESC Certification (ESC grantees)

Senior High School

  • For initial evaluation: photocopy of report card (grades up to at least the 3rd grading period), PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy), three (3) 2"x2" photos, and long brown expandable envelope
  • Upon official admission: original report card, original PSA Birth Certificate, original Good Moral Certificate, and original Form 137 / SF10
  • Signed parental / legal guardian consent
  • For applicable scholars: ESC Certificate, TLC Transfer Form, or voucher certificate (SHSVP / PUB-QVR / QVA)

Requirements may vary according to grade level and scholarship category. The authenticated student / parent portal provides the applicant's checklist and status. Please confirm special cases with the Admissions Office.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Moreh Academy admission process.

How do I start an application?

Select Enroll now or Start enrollment to open the secure portal. New enrollees can create an account and complete the required student information.

Are admission requirements the same for every grade?

No. Requirements depend on the applicant's grade level. Some transfer or scholarship documents apply only to eligible applicants.

What should Senior High applicants submit first?

For initial evaluation, submit photocopies of the report card (up to at least the 3rd grading period) and PSA Birth Certificate, together with the required photos and envelope. Original documents are required upon official admission.

Where can I view the School Fee Schedule?

The School Fee Schedule is not displayed on this public homepage. It is available only inside the authenticated student / parent account. Fee assessment is handled by the School Cashier.

Which Senior High clusters are offered?

Moreh Academy offers STEM, ASSH, Business and Entrepreneurship (BE), ICT Support and Computer Programming Technologies (ICT), and Hospitality and Tourism (HT).

Who can I contact for admission questions?

Contact the Admissions Office using the phone number or email listed below.
