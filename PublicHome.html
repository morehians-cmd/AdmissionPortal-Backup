Admission Requirements

Prepare for your next step.

Requirements depend on the learner's grade level and applicant category. Use this guide as a starting point, then check the Admission Portal for the requirements that apply to your application.

Nursery · Kinder 1

Preschool

  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Three (3) 2"×2" photos, white background, with nametag
  • Long brown expandable envelope, clean and free of writings or markings
  • Original parental consent, signed by the parent or legal guardian

Preschool applicants undergo assessment and evaluation.

Kinder 2 · Grades 1–6

Elementary

  • Original official report card
  • Original Form 137 / Permanent Record
  • Original certificate
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Three (3) 2"×2" photos, white background, with nametag
  • Long brown expandable envelope
  • Original parental consent, signed by the parent or legal guardian
Grades 7–10

Junior High School

  • Original official report card
  • Original Form 137 / SF10
  • Original certificate
  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Three (3) 2"×2" photos, white background, with nametag
  • Long brown expandable envelope
  • Original parental consent, signed by the parent or legal guardian

TLC Transfer Form or ESC Certification may apply to eligible scholars.

Grades 11–12

Senior High School

For initial evaluation: report card photocopy (up to at least the 3rd grading period), PSA Birth Certificate photocopy, three (3) 2"×2" photos, and a long brown expandable envelope.

For completion upon official admission: original report card, original PSA Birth Certificate, original Good Moral Certificate, and original Form 137 / SF10.

Parental consent and applicable transfer, ESC, or voucher documents may also be required. Confirm your checklist in the portal.

Important: Additional or conditional documents may apply based on grade level, learner history, and applicant category. The authenticated portal checklist is the reference for your application. For preschool and early-grade age or readiness rules, follow the current Admissions Office assessment instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you begin.

These answers explain the current portal process. For applicant-specific instructions, use the Admission Portal or contact the school.

How do I begin an admission application?

Open the Moreh Academy Admission Portal and follow the registration or sign-in instructions shown there.

Which documents should I prepare?

Requirements vary by grade level and applicant category. Review the Admission Requirements section and confirm your personal checklist inside the portal.

Are the requirements the same for every grade?

No. Preschool, Elementary, Junior High School, and Senior High School have different document requirements. Some transfer or scholarship documents apply only to eligible applicants.

Where can I see the School Fee Schedule?

The School Fee Schedule is not published on this public homepage. It is available only inside the authenticated student or parent account. The School Cashier is responsible for assessing fees.

Which Senior High School clusters are offered?

Moreh Academy's configured clusters are STEM; Arts, Social Sciences, and Humanities (ASSH); Business and Entrepreneurship (BE); ICT Support and Computer Programming Technologies (ICT); and Hospitality and Tourism (HT).

Who can help if I have a question about my application?

Contact the Admissions Office using the official phone or email details in the Contact section below.
